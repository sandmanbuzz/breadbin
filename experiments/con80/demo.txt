$ cat README
Breadbin is a Unix-like operating system for the Commodore 64. It has processes,
pipes, a shell, a filesystem and a block cache, and it replaces the KERNAL
entirely: the interrupt entry, keyboard, screen, serial bus and channels are its
own. Programs are written in 6502 assembly today; a compact VM and a small
compiled BASIC and C are planned. The target machines are a stock C64 with a
1541, an expanded one with a REU, and an Ultimate, in that order of ambition.
